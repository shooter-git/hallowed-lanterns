# Hallowed Lanterns — the story

> Every October night at the farm, the lanterns wake when the sun goes down. Each carved light
> keeps a little of the warmth of whoever carved it. Tonight one small flame — **the Ember** —
> slips out of its jack-o'-lantern and decides to show you everything before the night is over.

You follow the Ember from sunset to the Wishing Tree, where every light ends up. The chapters run
in the order the sky darkened in the photos: golden sunset in the jack-o'-lantern arbor, blue hour
in the candle grove, deep blue in the lantern tunnel, full night everywhere after.

The whole walk takes 20–30 minutes at an easy pace. Nobody is rushed: you can stop anywhere, and
once you've reached the Wishing Tree you can return to any chapter.

## The Ember

A living candle flame about the size of a fist. It bobs, waits for you, and gets curious about
whatever you look at. It never speaks. It *chimes*: a small five-note figure that is also the
soundtrack's main theme, played by a different instrument in every chapter (music box, celesta,
zither, cello, glockenspiel, sleigh bells, whale-song choir, nylon guitar). When you lose sight of
it, you can hear where it is.

## I. The Arbor of Faces — sunset

The walk begins at sunset in front of a great wall of stacked hay bales with the night's name in
big black letters, heirloom pumpkins (orange, white, dusty pink) piled on bales at its foot and a
rope of purple and orange lights strung on white stakes in front. Then into the arbor: a gravel lane under a long pergola of hundreds of carved jack-o'-lanterns, rows on rows of faces
overhead and along the barbed-wire fence. The sun is going down behind the field. Low fog glows
violet and peach, and purple and orange path lights run along both edges.

- As the sun slips, the faces **light up in a wave** that runs ahead of you down the lane.
- Look at a face and it notices you. Tap it and it flares and chuckles (a warm wooden knock and
  a chime).
- Near the end of the lane, one lantern sputters and its flame hops out: **the Ember**. It looks
  at you, waits, and darts off into the trees.

*Music — "Sundown":* music box and warm strings in a slow 6/8. *Around you:* the last birds of the
day giving way to the first crickets, gravel under your feet, distant happy voices.

## II. The Candle Grove — blue hour

The path enters the woods. Hundreds of carved pumpkins **hang in the branches**, with candles,
stars, moons and owls carved through their skins, and the sky is that deep blue just after
sunset. Fog rolls ankle-deep along the path, lit red from below, and parts around your feet as
you walk.

- The Ember plays **hide and seek**. It hides inside a hanging pumpkin, which glows brighter and
  hums. Find it three times and it leads you on.

*Music — "Blue Hour":* harp and celesta arpeggios over a soft choir. *Around you:* leaves in the
wind, crickets, the hush of the fog.

## III. The Lantern Garden — nightfall

A white archway opens into a tunnel of **red lanterns with gold tassels**, above reed fences
wound with warm fairy lights. At the end the trees open onto the **Lantern Sky**: one enormous
tree hung with hundreds of lanterns, swaying together.

Beneath it is a small lounge: a hay-bale sofa draped in a dragon throw, red cushions, and pumpkins
on wooden pallets. **It's the one place you can sit down.** When you do, the music settles, the
crickets come closer, and the lanterns overhead sway as one in a warm breeze.

- The Ember races ahead and lights lanterns as it passes. Tap a tassel to set its lantern
  swinging.
- Along a reed fence strung with fairy lights stands a **giant owl**: a pumpkin head with huge round
  eyes, a body of glowing pumpkins carved with feathers, and enormous wings of pale blue feathers.
  Tap it and it hoots, blinks and ruffles its wings.

*Music — "Lantern Song":* plucked zither and bamboo flute, pentatonic and unhurried. *Around
you:* rustling reeds, soft wind chimes.

## IV. The Pasture of Giants — deep night

Through a gap in the fence onto an open pasture under the stars, where a rope line of red and
amber lights runs along the edge. Three **enormous glowing longhorns** stand here: one grazing,
one lying down, one standing tall. They breathe, slowly.

- Walk close and the standing giant **lowers its great head to look at you**. The Ember settles
  between its horns.
- Tap one and it answers with a deep, gentle low that turns into a cello note. The field lights
  pulse with their breathing.

Past the herd, in a clearing ringed by trees, there's a **picture show**: a screen above a stage
of hay bales, white benches, strings of purple and blue globe lights overhead, green lights along
the bales. Sit on a bench and watch the film — a shadow-puppet tale of the Ember's own night.

*Music — "Gentle Giants":* low cello and horn in open fifths, big and still. *Around you:* an open
field of crickets and the quiet hum of the night.

## V. Sweet Hollow — candy land

On the way in: **the Web**. Stretchy white cobweb glows violet under blacklight — walls of it, a
ceiling of it, strands everywhere — with pumpkins wrapped in web and tiny neon spiders on them. A
giant pumpkin spider with pink spiral eyes watches from the middle, and a neon-green praying mantis
turns its head to follow you. The Ember flies straight into the web and gets stuck: tap the web to
free it.


Under an old oak washed in violet light: a giant jack-o'-lantern candy bucket with a glowing
handle, towers of candy corn, rainbow pumpkins stacked on skewers, gummy-bear pumpkins, lollipop
pumpkins, and rows of letter pumpkins.

- The letter pumpkins are **a xylophone**: tap them to play. Play the Ember's five notes and the
  whole hollow bursts into sprinkles.
- Gummy bears wobble and squeak. The Ember dives into the bucket and pops out again.

*Music — "Sugar Rush":* pizzicato strings and glockenspiel, playful.

## VI. October Snow

Then, impossibly, **it begins to snow**. A snowman made of striped white-and-green gourds raises
his corn-cob mug in a toast as you come near. Icicle lights and glowing snowflakes hang in the
trees. Everything goes hushed, and you can see your breath.

- Catch snowflakes (tap them); each one rings a tiny bell.

*Music — "First Snow":* celesta and sleigh bells, very quiet. *Around you:* the muffled hush of
snow, soft crunching steps.

## VII. The Deep

The snowflakes slow, stop, and start to **rise**: they're bubbles. Blue light pours in and you
are walking along the sea floor. **Pumpkin jellyfish** drift overhead trailing glowing ribbons,
pufferfish peer out from the branches, coral glows along a path of blue lights, and everything
sounds far away, as sound does underwater.

Halfway along: **the Kraken's Wreck**. Giant glowing orange tentacles rise out of the sea floor
around a great inflatable pumpkin; one arches over the broken hull of a ship; pumpkin-headed
skeleton pirates sit in a rowboat. Tap a tentacle and it curls. Further on lies the **treasure**:
a huge open chest heaped with gold on a low platform, skeletons guarding it, palm trees made of
pumpkins. Tap the chest and it creaks open — then sit a while beside it.

At the end, the **Keeper of the Deep** rises from waves of light: a giant octopus with a pumpkin
for a head and ember-orange eyes. It turns to look at you.

- **Wave to it** (tap it) and it waves a tentacle back.
- Jellyfish pulse brighter as you pass beneath them. Tap a pufferfish and it puffs up.

*Music — "Where the Lanterns Swim":* whale-song pads and a slow choir that swells when the Keeper
rises.

## VIII. The Marigold Garden — finale

The water drains away into marigold-scented mist. An **ofrenda** glows teal and gold: a pumpkin
skull with heart-shaped eyes under a crescent moon, monarch butterflies, papel picado, marigold
garlands, fruit, and rows of candles.

- **Light a candle for someone you remember.** Monarch butterflies lift from the marigolds.
  Candles stay lit for everyone in your group.

Beside the ofrenda, in green fog, stands a **giant guitar** covered in carved pumpkins — roses and
sugar skulls glowing red, a rim of little pink and purple lights — with six neon-green strings
rising high into the mist. Tap the strings to play them. (The Ember's song can be played on them
too.)

Beyond it is **the Wishing Tree**, with white branches covered in coral and teal leaves. Every
leaf is a wish. Along the boxwood hedge, under a marigold garland, a black-clothed table with a
little lamp, pencils and leaf cards is where people write them.

- **Write a wish at the table and hang your own.** It's kept for your group: everyone who opens this object sees the tree
  grow, one leaf at a time.
- The Ember settles on a branch and becomes a leaf of light. The lanterns in every chapter glow
  once, all together, and the night fades gently out.

*Music — "Remember Me":* nylon guitar and strings, ending on the full "Hallowed Lanterns" theme.

## Together

Everyone in the session walks the same night. Other people appear as their own small lantern-light
in their colour (like the glow bracelets in the photos), with their name. Candles at the ofrenda and
wishes on the tree are shared and kept. If people are in different chapters, a soft chime tells you
where they've gone.
